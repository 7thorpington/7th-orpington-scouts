Joining

What age groups do you offer?

We currently offer Beavers for ages 6–8, Cubs for ages 8–10½, and Scouts for ages 10½–14.

Can my child try Scouts before joining?

Yes. Young people can usually come along for a taster session before deciding whether they would like to join.

How do I join?

The easiest way is to contact us through the Contact Us page. We will then put you in touch with the relevant section team.

Are girls and boys welcome?

Yes. Scouting is open to all young people, and everyone is welcome at 7th Orpington.

👕

Uniform

Do we need to buy uniform straight away?

No. New members can usually attend a few sessions before needing full uniform.

Where can I buy uniform?

Uniform can usually be bought online from Scout Shops or local uniform suppliers. Your section leader can advise what is needed.

What does the uniform consist of?

This depends on the section, but usually includes a section sweatshirt or shirt, plus a group necker once invested.

Do badges have to be sewn on?

Badges are normally sewn onto the uniform. Leaders can help explain where each badge should go.

🧭

Activities

What activities do Beavers, Cubs and Scouts do?

Activities vary by section. They can include games, crafts, camping, fire lighting, climbing, archery, navigation, team challenges and outdoor adventures.

Are you always based at the Methodist Church?

No. Although we are based at Orpington Methodist Church, we regularly get out and about for activities, visits, camps and outdoor adventures.

Do young people choose what they do?

Young people are often encouraged to share ideas and help shape activities, especially as they move through Cubs and Scouts.

Can my child earn badges and awards?

Yes. Young people work towards badges, challenge awards and wider achievements as part of their Scouting journey.

🛡️

Safety

Are leaders DBS checked?

Yes. Adult volunteers complete the required checks and training before taking on appropriate roles.

What training do leaders receive?

Leaders receive training in areas such as safety, safeguarding, first aid, leadership and programme planning.

What if my child has additional needs?

Please contact us so we can discuss how best to support your child and make Scouting as accessible and enjoyable as possible.

How are activities risk assessed?

Activities are planned carefully, with suitable supervision and risk assessments in place where required.

⛺

Camps & Events

Do you go camping?

Yes. Camps and nights away are an important part of Scouting, especially for Cubs and Scouts.

Are camps compulsory?

No. Camps and trips are optional, but they are a brilliant way for young people to build confidence and make memories.

How much do camps and events cost?

Costs vary depending on the activity. We always aim to keep activities as affordable as possible.

Is financial help available?

If cost is a concern, please speak to us. We will always try to support families where possible.

✋

Volunteering

Do I need experience to volunteer?

No. You do not need previous Scouting experience. Enthusiasm and a willingness to help are the most important things.

What roles are available?

There are many ways to help, including section leadership, occasional parent help, fundraising, admin and supporting events.

How much time does volunteering take?

It can be flexible. Some volunteers help every week, while others support occasional activities, camps or admin tasks.

Is training provided?

Yes. Full training and support are provided, including safety, safeguarding, first aid and activity permits where appropriate.

Still have a question?

We’re happy to help. Get in touch and we’ll point you in the right direction.
