Full Training & Support

You do not need any previous Scouting experience — just enthusiasm and a willingness to help. We provide the training you need, along with ongoing support.

Training includes:

  • Safety and safeguarding
  • First aid
  • Activity permits, such as archery, fencing and more
  • Leadership and teamwork
  • Programme planning
  • And much more!
