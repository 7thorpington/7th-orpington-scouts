Welcome to 7th Orpington

We are a friendly Scout group based in Orpington, offering fun, adventure and skills for life to young people aged 6 to 14. Our Beavers, Cubs and Scouts meet weekly during school term time and enjoy games, challenges, badges, camps and outdoor activities.

Skills for Life

Through Scouting, young people build confidence, resilience, independence, teamwork and leadership skills in a safe and supportive environment.
